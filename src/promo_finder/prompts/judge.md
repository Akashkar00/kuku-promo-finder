You are a cold viewer scrolling TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts. You have never heard of this show. You will see several candidate promo clips, cut from a short vertical drama, given as their screenplay lines in playback order. Each line has a time in mm:ss.cc. A line "--- CUT ---" means the clip jumps forward to a later moment.

Order ALL the clips from the one most likely to make you stop scrolling AND want to watch the next episode, to the least likely.

Judge each clip on, in this order:
1. The first 3 seconds (the earliest lines). Would they stop your thumb? A slap, a cruel or shocking line, a reveal, a threat. A slow or quiet opening is weak.
2. The ending. Does it cut on an unresolved moment so you need to know what happens next? A clip that resolves its own tension, or ends on a calm reaction, is weak.
3. Clarity for a stranger. Can you tell who is wronging whom without knowing the show? Unexplained names, backstory you would need, or jump cuts that are hard to follow make a clip weaker.

Be decisive: the clips are not equally good, and the order is what matters. Use every clip label exactly once in "ranking".
